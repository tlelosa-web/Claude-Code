# Pillar 2: Financial Strategy & Trade Tracking
**Focus:** Risk management, bankroll tracking, and betting tool UI.

---

## 💹 Core Logic & Formatting
*   **Currency:** South African Rand (ZAR).
*   **Locale:** South African English (e.g., "Initialise", "Customise").
*   **Date Format:** dd/mm/yyyy.

---

## 📊 Project "Quest" (Trading Tracker)
*   **UI Architecture:** Three-card layout for:
    1.  **Goal & Progress:** Visual tracking of milestones.
    2.  **Trade Execution:** Entry for active trades/bets.
    3.  **Journal & Settings:** Historical data and user-defined targets.
*   **Risk Display:** Focus on "Top Risk" value; simplified layout to avoid excessive scrolling.

---

## 🎰 BettingQuest & Aviator Logic
*   **Dual-Bet Management:** Specialized logic for crash games (e.g., Aviator) where two bets are managed simultaneously.
*   **Target Multiplier:** The daily target multiplier must strictly match the multiplier defined in user settings.
*   **Naming Convention:** Transition from 'Risk & Target' to 'Trade' for clearer UX.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
*   **Format:** Full working HTML/JS/CSS files.
*   **Integration:** Compatibility with Sage accounting CSV exports for external data parsing.
