### 3.1 ECSA -- Engineering Council of South Africa (ecsa.co.za)

#### Registration Guidance

ECSA registration significantly boosts employability and is often a requirement for senior engineering roles.

| Registration Category | Requirement | Tebello's Status |
|----------------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Candidate Engineering Technologist | National Diploma in Engineering + ECSA assessment | ELIGIBLE to apply |
| Professional Engineering Technologist (Pr.Techn.Eng) | 3 years post-qualification experience + ECSA assessment | STRONGLY ELIGIBLE (14+ years experience, current Operations Foreman role adds practical leadership evidence) |
| Professional Engineer (Pr.Eng) | BEng/BSc Eng 4-year degree | Not applicable (National Diploma route) |

#### Recommended Action Plan

1. **Apply for Candidate Engineering Technologist registration immediately** if not yet registered. This requires:
   - Certified copy of National Diploma
   - Academic transcript
   - Completed ECSA application form
   - Registration fee (approximately R600-800)
2. **Apply for Professional Engineering Technologist (Pr.Techn.Eng)** if you have sufficient documented training reports. This requires:
   - 3 years minimum post-qualification practical training/experience
   - Completed ECSA Training Report
   - Interview/assessment
3. **Benefits of ECSA registration:**
   - Required for many government and parastatal engineering roles (Eskom, Transnet, PRASA, SANRAL)
   - Significantly increases salary negotiation leverage
   - Opens doors to consulting engineering opportunities
