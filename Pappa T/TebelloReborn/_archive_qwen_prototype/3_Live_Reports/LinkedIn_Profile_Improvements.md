## 7. Profile Photo & Banner

### Profile Photo

**Requirements:**
- Professional headshot, shoulders up, clear face
- Neutral or blurred background (not a busy environment)
- Business casual attire (collared shirt or suit jacket -- no need for full formal wear, but no t-shirts)
- Natural lighting, facing the camera, genuine smile
- High resolution (minimum 400x400 pixels, ideally 1000x1000)
- Recent photo (within the last 2 years)

**What to Avoid:**
- Selfies, group photos, or cropped photos with other people
- Sunglasses, hats, or anything obscuring the face
- Low-quality or pixelated images
- Overly casual settings (braai, holiday, etc.)

**South African Context:** A clean, professional headshot in a collared shirt or suit jacket against a neutral background is the standard for engineering and supply chain professionals in SA. If possible, have the photo taken by a professional photographer (costs R500-R1,500 at most studios). Alternatively, use a smartphone with portrait mode in good natural lighting against a plain wall.

### Banner / Background Photo

The banner is the large image behind the profile photo. Most profiles use the default grey gradient -- replacing this with a relevant image immediately differentiates the profile.

**Recommended Banner Options (in order of preference):**

1. **Power Station / Industrial Facility:** A high-quality image of a power station, industrial plant, or manufacturing facility. This immediately signals heavy industry and power sector experience. Search for royalty-free images on Unsplash or Pexels using terms like "power station," "industrial plant," or "manufacturing facility."

2. **Project Engineering Scene:** An image showing engineering drawings, a project site, or a team in a professional setting (hard hats, site visit context).

3. **Supply Chain / Logistics Image:** A warehouse, logistics operation, or supply chain visual.

4. **South African Energy Landscape:** A wide shot of the South African Highveld with power station infrastructure in the distance.

**Banner Specifications:**
- Size: 1584 x 396 pixels (LinkedIn recommended)
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Keep it professional and relevant -- avoid text-heavy banners

**Where to Find Free Images:**
- Unsplash.com (search: "power station," "industrial," "manufacturing")
- Pexels.com (search: "engineering," "supply chain")
- Pixabay.com (search: "energy," "project management")
