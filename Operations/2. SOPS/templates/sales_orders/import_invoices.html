CSV Format Requirements

The CSV file should be exported from Sage's Customer Invoices report (CustomerInvoicesReport.csv).

Expected columns:

  • Date — Invoice date
  • Document No. — Invoice number, used to match/upsert
  • Customer Ref.
  • Customer
  • Sales Rep
  • Due Date
  • Exclusive
  • VAT
  • Total Selling
  • Total Outstanding

Rows with a missing Document No. or an unparseable Date/Due Date are skipped, not imported.
