## DO / DON'T

| DO | DON'T |
|----|-------|
| Personalize the first line with their company/industry | Send generic "Dear Sir/Madam" blasts |
| Keep it to 8-12 lines | Write essays — recruiters skim |
| Attach PDF, not Word | Attach .docx files (formatting breaks) |
| Send during business hours (08:00-17:00) | Send at midnight or weekends |
| Follow up once at 3-5 days | Follow up more than twice |
| Log every email in the tracker | Send and forget — track everything |
